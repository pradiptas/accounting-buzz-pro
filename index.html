Common questions

A few things worth knowing.

What kinds of businesses do you support?

Accounting Buzz is designed for individuals, owner-led companies, startups, and growing small businesses. We support technology, real estate, healthcare, manufacturing, retail and e-commerce, professional services, food service, construction and trades, and personal-service businesses.

Can you clean up books that are behind?

Yes. We can assess the current file, identify missing reconciliations or unsupported balances, and scope a catch-up or cleanup project before moving into ongoing monthly support.

Do you work with my CPA?

Absolutely. The goal is a smooth handoff: clear schedules, reconciled accounts, supporting documents, and a concise list of anything that still needs CPA judgment.

What tax services do you provide?

We support personal and corporate income-tax preparation, GST/HST and applicable provincial sales-tax reporting, tax-ready bookkeeping, planning conversations, and filing coordination. Exact scope depends on your entity, circumstances, and jurisdiction.

Can you help with a CRA letter or notice?

Yes. We can help interpret the request, organize the supporting records, correct related bookkeeping issues, and coordinate the next steps and deadlines. Representation or specialized dispute work is scoped separately where appropriate.

What does payroll support include?

Support can include recurring pay runs, wages and vacation pay, deductions, employee records, CRA remittance routines, payroll reporting, and T4 and T4 Summary year-end preparation. The exact workflow depends on your pay schedule, team, and existing payroll platform.

Can you work with our existing accounting software?

Often, yes. We start by reviewing your current file and workflow. If the platform is workable, we improve the setup around it; if a change would materially improve accuracy or efficiency, we explain the reason and plan the transition.

Can you set up a new business?

We can help establish the accounting system, reporting structure, financial workflow, and incorporation coordination. Legal formation advice remains with an appropriately qualified legal professional.

Do you provide cross-border tax services?

No. Accounting Buzz is focused on domestic bookkeeping, tax compliance, payroll, reporting, and business advisory services.

How are services priced?

Pricing is based on the volume, complexity, cleanup required, reporting needs, and service cadence. After an initial review, we’ll recommend a clearly defined project fee or recurring engagement.

How will we work together?

We agree on a secure document flow, a compliance and close calendar, and clear points of contact. You’ll know what we need, when reports are coming, and which open questions need your input.

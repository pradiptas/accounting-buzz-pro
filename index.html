Common questions

A few things worth knowing.

Still wondering whether we’re a fit? Send a note and tell us what’s happening in your books.

support.acctbuzzpro@gmail.com ↗
Who do you help?

We support individuals, families, sole proprietors, and small to medium-sized businesses. Engagements can be one-time—such as a tax return—or ongoing, such as daily bookkeeping and monthly reconciliations.

What bookkeeping work do you handle?

We can organize daily income and expenses, categorize transactions, manage vendor bills and customer invoices, track payments, and keep your financial records current.

What tax services do you provide?

We support personal and corporate income-tax preparation, GST/HST and applicable provincial sales-tax filings, tax-ready bookkeeping, and filing coordination. Exact scope depends on your circumstances and jurisdiction.

Can you clean up books that are behind?

Yes. We can assess the current file, identify missing transactions or reconciliations, and scope the cleanup needed before tax filing or ongoing bookkeeping begins.

Do you work with my CPA?

Yes. We prepare reconciled accounts, supporting documents, and a clear year-end package so your CPA can focus on review and tax strategy.

Can you help me start or scale a business?

Yes. We can help coordinate business registration, establish the bookkeeping and tax workflow, and set up or maintain your business website. Legal formation advice remains with an appropriately qualified legal professional.

How are services priced?

Pricing depends on transaction volume, filing requirements, cleanup needed, and whether support is one-time or recurring. After a short review, we’ll recommend a clearly defined fee and working plan.
